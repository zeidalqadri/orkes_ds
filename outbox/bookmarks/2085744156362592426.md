## Why

The durable idea is not a new model or framework: automate a narrow, repetitive, measurable work unit only after its manual path is known; keep a human in the external-facing or irreversible decision loop. That maps directly to recurring sec- operations (document triage, status reconciliation, notification delivery checks, bounded portal diagnostics) and to arbos- maintenance reporting/triage.

It does **not** justify giving a general-purpose agent broad browser, shell, inbox, or credential access. Existing fleet incidents make unattended provider routing, restart behavior, stale state, and resource ceilings higher-priority than adding another autonomous runtime. Marketing/creator acquisition tactics and claimed revenue are outside the platform mission.

## Concrete adopt/adapt proposals

1. **Build a candidate-task ledger, not an OpenClaw deployment.** For each arbos-/sec- workflow, record trigger, observed weekly volume, manual minutes, data class, side-effect level, required approval, deterministic fallback, and success/error metric. Admit only tasks that are repetitive, bounded, and independently reversible.
2. **Pilot one sec- task in shadow mode for seven days:** classify WebDAV/portal exception records into `auto-close | needs-review | retry`, with no action taken. Compare precision, operator corrections, retries, elapsed time, token spend, and RAM against the current deterministic path. Promote only if it beats the baseline and stays within ceilings.
3. **Use approval queues for external or state-changing work.** Draft supplier/assignment notifications, portal retries, or remediation commands into an auditable queue; a deterministic executor performs only explicit approvals. No direct browser automation, messaging, PM2 control, or credential use from a generative agent.
4. **Keep access capability-scoped.** Any future browser or remote runner receives per-job inputs, read-only/local fixture scope first, command/network allowlists, time/resource caps, redacted logs, and expiry. Do not introduce a persistent general agent gateway.
5. **Reject revenue as the metric.** Score pilots on correct completions per operator minute, correction rate, failure containment, model/tool cost, and host-memory impact. Stop a pilot on sustained regression or any scope violation.

## Disposition

**Adapt now:** task inventory + a single shadow-mode sec- pilot.

**Do not adopt:** OpenClaw runtime, persistent private gateway, broad browser access, autonomous outbound communication, creator-growth playbook, or revenue claims.
