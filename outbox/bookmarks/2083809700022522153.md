## Summary

freebuff (`npm i -g freebuff`) is a free terminal-based AI coding agent using open-source models (DeepSeek V4 Pro, MiMo 2.5 Pro, MiniMax M3). Ad-supported. 5-10x faster than Claude at 3-5x tokens/sec. Built-in web research and browser use. No subscription, no config.

## Verdict: LOW relevance

## Relevance Assessment

| Domain | Relevance | Why |
|--------|-----------|-----|
| arbos- | LOW | Competitive product to Claude-based agent loop. Interesting architecture (open-source models, ad-supported pricing, built-in browser) but **privacy concerns disqualify it for production use**: ad personalization scans prompts/messages, some models may train on submitted data. Contains procurement-sensitive data — non-starter. |
| sec- | LOW | Browser capabilities could theoretically aid recon, but freebuff is a coding agent, not a security tool. No auth testing, proxy support, or pentesting features. Data collection policies are incompatible with client pentest confidentiality. |

## Concrete Proposals

### Adopt: None
Privacy/confidentiality concerns block adoption. Both arbos and sec handle sensitive data (procurement details, pentest findings) that cannot flow through an ad-supported service that scans prompts for personalization.

### Adapt: None
The open-source model pricing strategy (free tier → ad-supported) is an interesting business model pattern but not actionable for our architecture. The speed claims (5-10x Claude) are likely overstated for reasoning-heavy agent loops.

## Key Risk: Data Privacy
- "We may analyze prompts and messages—including pasted content—to personalize ads"
- "Only when a model or feature says data may be used for AI training" — ambiguous, model-dependent
- Procurement bids, supplier data, and security findings are confidential. Cannot risk exposure.
