## Concrete Adopt / Adapt Proposals

1. **ADOPT — allowlisted listing register**  
   Create a versioned register for only verified, relevant directories. Store: site, target property, canonical URL, owner, account/recovery location, submission date, editorial policy, live URL, and review date. Begin with a maximum of three manually approved candidates; do not infer validity from the image's DR labels.

2. **ADAPT — canonical product-profile packet**  
   Maintain one approved, short profile for `harga.work/vX` and `harga.roowang.com/vX`: accurate scope, distinct portal coverage, logo/assets, support contact, canonical URL, and privacy-safe screenshots. Reuse it manually so listings cannot blur the SmartGEP versus non-SmartGEP separation.

3. **ADAPT — approval-gated, non-generative workflow**  
   Let arbos prepare a draft submission packet and detect stale/broken listings, but require an operator approval before creating an account, accepting terms, publishing, or granting OAuth. Store no third-party passwords in agent state; use an existing credential manager/account owner.

4. **MEASURE — six-week experiment with an exit rule**  
   For each approved listing, record indexed status, live canonical link, referral visits, qualified inbound enquiries, and correction effort. Retain only sources that produce a measurable referral/discovery signal without spam, policy, or maintenance cost; remove or ignore the rest. Do not use DR as the promotion metric.

5. **SKIP — mass “DR25+” automation**  
   Do not build a scraper or browser automation that submits to all 18 sites. It is unrelated to sec portal operations and creates external-action, account, and reputation risk without a proven return.
